# 🥤 Food & Beverage Survey Analytics – CodeX Energy Drink  

---

## 📌 Project Overview  
Performed **end-to-end survey analytics** for the CodeX energy-drink study:  
- Prepared & joined **3 survey tables** (respondents, cities, responses)  
- Cleaned & validated raw survey data  
- Conducted **exploratory & demographic analysis**  
- Produced **consumer-preference and competitor insights**  
- Delivered **data-backed recommendations** for marketing & product teams  

---

## 🌟 About CodeX  
- **Origin:** German beverage company, newly launched in India  
- **Survey Reach:** Conducted in **10 Indian cities**  
- **Respondents:** **10,000+ participants**  
- **Survey Focus:**  
  - Consumer behavior (purchase habits, pricing, packaging)  
  - Feedback on energy drinks  
  - Competitor benchmarking  

The **Marketing Team’s Goal** → Convert raw survey data into **actionable insights** to shape marketing, sales, and product development strategy.  

---

## 🛠️ Tasks Performed  
1. **Data Familiarization**  
   - Downloaded dataset (.csv) and manually validated for errors  

2. **Data Integration in Power BI**  
   - Created measures: total responses, avg taste rating, gender counts  
   - Generated city-wise respondent counts using MySQL export  

3. **Exploratory Drafting**  
   - Drafted initial ideas in Word file  
   - Brainstormed **marketing strategies** (influencer outreach, campaigns, etc.)  

4. **Visualization & Dashboards**  
   - Designed multiple Power BI report pages with visuals  

5. **Design & Presentation**  
   - Selected **green-themed color palette** for consistency  
   - Built **final PPT in Canva** with dashboard screenshots

## 🔎 Problem Statements & Insights  

### 📊 Demographic Insights  
- **Gender:** Out of **10,000 respondents**, **6,038 (~60%) were male** → Higher male preference for energy drinks  
- **Age Group:**  
  - **15–30 years old = 70%** of total respondents  
  - Energy drinks are overwhelmingly popular among **youth**  
- **Marketing Reach:** Online Ads engaged **3,373 respondents (15–30 age group)** → most effective youth channel  

---

### 🛒 Consumer Preferences  
- **Preferred Ingredient:** ☕ **Caffeine** (highest preference), followed by vitamins  
- **Packaging:**  
  - **Compact & Portable Cans** (most demanded)  
  - Innovative Bottle Designs (secondary preference)  

---

### 🏆 Competition Analysis  
- **Market Leaders:**  
  - 🥇 **Cola Coka**  
  - 🥈 **Bepsi**  
- **Reason for Preference:** Strong **brand reputation & visibility**  

---

### 📣 Marketing Channels & Awareness  
- **Most Effective Channel:** **Online Ads** → High youth penetration + cost efficiency  
- **Brand Penetration:**  
  - Out of **980 aware respondents**, only **219 (22%)** had a **positive perception** of CodeX  
  - **Average taste rating = 3.3/5** → industry benchmark  

---

### 🌍 City-wise Insights  
- **Neutral + Negative feedback > Positive** in many cities → Opportunity for **brand perception improvement**  
- Focus areas: **Ahmedabad, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Lucknow, Mumbai, Pune**  

---

### 🛒 Purchase Behavior  
- **Purchase Location:** Supermarkets dominate as the preferred channel  
- **Consumption Situations:**  
  - Sports / Exercise  
  - Studying & Late-night work  
- **Price Sensitivity:**  
  - **43% of respondents** prefer a price between **₹50–₹99**  
  - Some cities willing to pay up to **₹150**  
- **Packaging Influence:**  
  - **40% prefer no change**  
  - **39% open to limited edition packs**  

---

### 🧪 Product Development Insights  
- **Positive Brand Perception:** Only **22% (219 out of 980 aware consumers)** → Needs urgent improvement  
- **Taste Experience:** Matches industry average (not differentiating enough)  
- **Availability:** Critical gap → **20% purchase decisions are driven by availability alone**  

---

## ✅ Recommendations for CodeX  

1. **Product Improvements**  
   - Enhance **availability** in key markets  
   - Health-focused variants → natural ingredients, less sugar, more flavors  
   - Limited Edition Packaging for youth appeal  

2. **Pricing Strategy**  
   - Target Range: **₹50–₹150**  
   - City-specific strategy: Metro cities willing to pay higher  

3. **Marketing Campaigns**  
   - Bundle offers (e.g., 6-pack discounts)  
   - Festival-themed **gift packs**  
   - Leverage **influencer marketing** & **social media ads**  

4. **Brand Ambassadors & Channels**  
   - Social Media Influencers  
   - Online Retail Partnerships  
   - Creative Guerilla Marketing  

5. **Target Audience**  
   - **15–30 Age Group** → Social media first strategy  
   - Heavy focus on **digital channels & youth-centric campaigns**
